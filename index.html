Who we are

KJ Engineering & Consulting Pty. Ltd. is a Wollongong based structural engineering consultancy providing professional, reliable and practical engineering solutions across the greater Illawarra region. As a new business backed by the expertise of an experienced Chartered and Registered Engineer, we combine technical knowledge and sound engineering judgement with a modern, client-focused approach. We are committed to delivering safe, high-quality and cost-effective solutions tailored to each project's needs, while prioritising clear communication, personalised service and efficient project delivery. Our goal is to build lasting relationships with our clients through dependable advice, practical design solutions and a commitment to engineering excellence.

Who we work with

KJ Engineering & Consulting Pty. Ltd. works alongside homeowners, builders, architects, developers and other construction professionals to support projects of varying sizes and complexity. We understand that successful projects rely on coordination between everyone involved, and we aim to make the engineering component a seamless part of the process. Whether you are planning a residential project, managing a construction development or coordinating a broader design team, we work with you to address structural requirements and help move your project forward.

How a project runs

  1. Enquiry

    Client to provide site address, drawings and photos.

  2. Scope

    We provide a written proposal before any works start.

  3. Site inspection

    We inspect the existing site and conditions where required.

  4. Design & drawings

    We complete design calculations and provide structural drawings ready for your certifier and builder.

  5. Construction

    We undertake site inspections, answer RFIs and provide final certification.
